On a touch screen, use the pad under the board — or just swipe across the board itself.

Game modes

  • 🐍 Classic — walls kill. Traditional Snake.
  • ⏱️ Time Attack — race the clock; every snack buys seconds.
  • 🧱 Survival — bins keep appearing and the board closes in.
  • ♾️ Endless — no walls, you wrap around, and it never stops speeding up.

Difficulty changes real numbers: speed, how many snacks a level takes, how many bins appear and how long the clock runs.

Noodle wisdom

Hug the edges and always leave yourself an exit. He physically cannot reverse into his own neck, so turn early rather than late.
