What's inside

Everything is built from scratch — the 3D models are procedural geometry, the sounds are synthesised, the jokes are hand-written. Nothing is downloaded from anywhere.

🎮

A real 3D arena

Three.js renders a raised checkered board with chunky walls, soft shadows and a camera that gently follows the action without making you seasick.

🐍

An expressive noodle

Big eyes that track where he's going and whatever smells good. Six faces — content, hungry, chomping, panicking, hurt, and very much dead.

🍕

Eight ridiculous snacks

Pizza, burger, donut, banana, taco, fries, apple and cake — all modelled from primitives, all spinning and bobbing, all about to be eaten.

💬

Running commentary

Over ninety lines that escalate from "YUM!" to "ABSOLUTE UNIT" as the run gets absurd — and the restraint to stay quiet most of the time.

🔊

Cartoon sound

Wet chomps that pitch up with your streak, a level-up fanfare, a deflating game-over slide, and a comedy burp every seventh snack. Mute any time.

🛟

Runs anywhere

Phone, tablet or desktop. If your browser has no WebGL it quietly switches to the 2D renderer — same game, same noodle, same jokes.

Four ways to eat

Pick a mode and a difficulty from the main menu. Difficulty changes real numbers — speed, snacks per level, how many bins spawn, how long the clock runs.

🐍

Classic

Walls kill. Pure, traditional Snake with a level cap of ten.

⏱️

Time Attack

Race a countdown. Every snack buys you seconds. Run out and it's over.

🧱

Survival

Bins keep appearing. The board fills up around you. Don't hit one.

♾️

Endless

No walls — wrap to the other side. It never stops getting faster.

📱 Touch

A big thumb-friendly direction pad sits under the board on phones and tablets — or just swipe across the board itself. The layout is built so the board and the pad are always on screen together.

🎨 Themes

Four looks — Hungry Noodle, Spicy, Dessert Monster and Alien — that repaint the arena and the whole page. Your pick is remembered.
