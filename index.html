Controls

  • ↑↓←→Steer the snake
  • WASDSteer the snake
  • SpaceStart / pause / resume
  • PPause or resume
  • RRestart the run
  • MMute or unmute

On a touch screen, use the direction pad under the board — or just swipe across the board itself.

How it works

  • Your snake — it grows with every bite
  • Fruit — worth 10 × level points
  • Walls — touching one ends the run

Every 4 fruits bumps you up a level, to a maximum of level 10. Each level makes the snake move noticeably quicker.

Tip

Hug the edges and always leave yourself an exit. Reversing straight into your own neck is blocked, so turn early rather than late.
